A valuation visit is straightforward, and you do not need to stage your home. A little preparation does help the conversation.

## Before the visit

- Find your levy and rates statements, if you can.
- Note any improvements you have made, and roughly when.
- Think about what you hope to do next, even if you are not sure yet.

## During the visit

The visit takes about 45 minutes. We walk through the unit, then compare it with registered transfers in your complex. You leave with an indicative figure and a clear explanation of how it was reached.

## After the visit

There is no obligation. Many owners simply want to know where they stand. If you decide to sell, we can talk about the next step.

*Property values are indicative only and do not constitute a formal valuation.*
